# Empowering DeFi Through Capital-Efficient Lending

Our mission is to build the most capital-efficient lending protocol native to Polkadot Hub.

In 2024, the DeFi landscape faces a critical challenge: existing lending protocols often leave capital idle, rely on static interest rate models that fail to respond to real market conditions, and expose borrowers to inefficient liquidation processes that destroy value for both borrowers and the protocol.

## The Current State of DeFi Lending

The fundamental problem in today's lending ecosystem isn't the lack of protocols—it's the absence of capital efficiency and intelligent risk management. When a user wants to lend or borrow, they typically face:

- Static interest rate models that don't respond to market utilization
- Idle collateral that generates no yield while securing a loan
- Inefficient liquidation processes that destroy value for both borrowers and lenders
- Limited options for exotic tokens
- Loan pools that lack adequate incentive mechanisms to bootstrap and maintain stable liquidity

While existing solutions have made progress, none have successfully combined deep liquidity, optimal capital efficiency, and a seamless user experience into a single, powerful platform.

## The Kylix Solution

Kylix Finance is a lending protocol built natively on Polkadot Hub, designed around capital efficiency and intelligent risk management. Our protocol enables:

- Self-repaying loans, where deposited collateral is converted into an interest-bearing asset that automatically covers the debt position
- A polynomial interest rate model that incentivizes liquidity providers even when market utilization is low
- A queue-based liquidation marketplace, where liquidated collateral is made available to the community through a fair bidding process
- Collateral scoring and loan pools designed for capital efficiency

## Beyond Simple Lending

Kylix Finance is not just another lending pool - it's a comprehensive, capital-efficient lending infrastructure. Our protocol enables:

- Users to act as lenders, borrowers or liquidators
- Automated collection of protocol fees on every borrow and liquidation
- Self-repaying loans for the enjoyment of negative interest loans
- Collateral scoring and loan pools with new-generation interest rate design

## The Road Ahead

Kylix Finance is positioning itself at the forefront of capital-efficient, native Polkadot Hub lending. Our roadmap includes:

- Deepening integration with the Polkadot Hub ecosystem
- Advanced lending features including FIAT Lending Pools and Undercollateralised Loans
- Institutional-grade services and further integration with TradFi
