#### 1. What is Kylix?

- Kylix Finance is a lending protocol built natively on Polkadot Hub, designed to manage over-collateralized loans with best-in-class capital efficiency.

  Kylix combines self-repaying loans, a polynomial interest rate model, and a queue-based liquidation marketplace to maximize returns for lenders and minimize risk for the protocol. Everything is recorded natively on Polkadot Hub.

#### 2. Why build on Polkadot Hub?

- Polkadot Hub gives Kylix a secure, scalable foundation with native smart contract support, without the operational overhead of running and securing a separate chain.

  Building natively on Polkadot Hub lets Kylix focus entirely on capital efficiency and risk management — the core problems the protocol is designed to solve — instead of on cross-chain infrastructure.

#### 3. Which problems are you trying to solve?

1. The fundamental problem in today's DeFi lending ecosystem isn't the lack of protocols—it's the absence of capital efficiency and intelligent risk management. When a user wants to lend or borrow, they typically face:

   - Static interest rate models that don't respond to market utilization
   - Idle collateral that generates no yield while securing a loan
   - Inefficient liquidation processes that destroy value for both borrowers and lenders
   - Limited options for exotic tokens
   - Loan pools that lack adequate incentive mechanisms to bootstrap and maintain stable liquidity

   While existing solutions have made progress, none have successfully combined deep liquidity, optimal capital efficiency, and a seamless user experience into a single, powerful platform.

2. Decentralised finance (DeFi) lending platforms face significant challenges such as inefficient collateral liquidation systems and idle capital. In addition, many lending platforms lack adequate incentive mechanisms to bootstrap and maintain stable liquidity.

#### 5. How are liquidations executed?

- The Kylix marketplace uses a queue-based system to handle liquidations. As assets are liquidated, they are made available for purchase by community members through a bidding process. Bids are filled sequentially, starting with the smallest discount and progressing to the largest. This approach ensures a fair and efficient distribution of assets while maximizing returns for the protocol. In scenarios where bid liquidity in the queues of the liquidation markets is insufficient to cover a liquidation, Kylix seamlessly executes asset swaps over multiple paths through partnerships with external market makers.

#### 6. What are self-repaying loans, and how do they benefit users?

- Self-repaying loans are a specialised financial instrument where the deposited collateral is converted into an interest-bearing asset that generates income that is automatically used to cover the debt position. This structure allows borrowers to leverage assets supported by liquid staking platforms to hold a loan with negative interest rates.

#### 7. What exactly are polynomial interest rates, and why used?

- Kylix Finance has implemented an innovative interest rate model for its lending pools, designed to incentivize liquidity providers even when market utilization rates are low.

#### 11. What’s the reason for the native KLX token?

- The protocol is secured by the native KLX token. KLX is primarily a utility token. Validators need KLX to validate and are rewarded in KLX. Value is indirectly returned to validators through protocol fees. In addition, any borrow and liquidation executed on the platform results in the automatic collection of fees, which are collected by the Kylix treasury. A portion of the treasury is used to reward validators and a portion of the KLX tokens are burned, putting deflationary pressure on the network as borrowing and liquidation volume increases.

#### 12. How do users get the token to use the network?

Participants in this round will gain full access to the Kylix platform, including staking and governance privileges. For those unable to participate in the Polimec auction, the network will provide a public faucet on the platform, offering minimal KLX tokens to cover transaction fees.
